The method

How a project unfolds

Five movements, one point of contact, and a short update from us every week so you're never left guessing.

MOVEMENT I

Listen

A first conversation about your goals, your customers and what has or hasn't worked before. We write it up as a one-page brief you can correct and approve.

MOVEMENT II

Shape

We decide what each page needs to say and in what order, then sketch the structure. Nothing is polished yet, which keeps changes cheap and conversations honest.

MOVEMENT III

Make

Design and build happen together. You review working pages rather than static pictures, so what you approve is exactly what will launch.

MOVEMENT IV

Refine

The finishing pass: spacing, typography, motion, accessibility and speed, tested on real phones and slower connections until nothing feels off.

MOVEMENT V

Release

We launch, connect your domain, hand over every file and walk your team through the basics. A short support period follows while the site settles.
