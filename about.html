About the studio

A small studio with strong opinions.

Northpane exists because most websites are either loud or forgettable. We think there's a better place between the two: calm, precise and unmistakably yours.

We keep the studio deliberately small. The person you speak to at the start is the person who builds your site at the end, which means fewer handovers, fewer misunderstandings and better work.

We design for the visitor on a mid-range phone with an average connection, because that is who most websites are actually for.
